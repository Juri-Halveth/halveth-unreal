HALVETH PORTAL GARDEN 0.2.0

An independent native Unreal Engine world prototype: explore, read, craft,
enchant and build. Start HALVETHRealms.exe or the versioned installed shortcut
HALVETH Portal Garden 0.2.0. No Unreal Editor, browser, account or wallet is
needed to run the game. This is an unsigned Win64 Development prototype.
Release-specific validation is available with the GitHub download.

EXPLORE
WASD / mouse: move / look. Space: jump. Shift: run. Left Ctrl: dodge.
E near a material marker, book, guide or portal: gather / read / speak / travel.
R: return home. B: open the library. T: choose structure. G: build ahead.
Q: choose LOVE, SPARK or AEGIS. Left mouse: cast. L: quick LOVE.
I: select inventory item. F: use selected item.
1 / 2 / 3: Performance / Balanced / Epic. H: controls. F10: credits.
Escape: exit. Falling from an island returns you to the spawn point.

LIBRARY AND WORKSHOP
Six original texts contain fifteen pages. Tab: next text.
Page Up / Page Down: change page. 1 / 2 / 3: answer the recall prompt.
O: cycle the right-hand panel. C: choose recipe or enchantment. V: make it.
P: pack a crafted Lumen Draught into your healing-item pocket.
T: choose Ground / Workbench, Camp or Beacon.
N: select one of three council decisions. Z / X / Y: select its three routes.
F5: save. F9: reload the saved knowledge and inventories for this seed.
B / Escape: close the library.

For visible construction: T selects, B closes, face clear ground, then G
places the chosen structure. Materials are charged only for a successful
construction. A Ground / Workbench is the milestone for Camp and Beacon;
up to 32 constructions are recorded across the four realms.

First successful recall earns knowledge once. Reading time only records
engagement; there is no minimum wait and it is not a comprehension score.
Crafting spends materials and earns practical skill. The three recipes are
Lumen Draught, Ward Ink and Binding Resin. The workshop lists their sources,
costs and your available quantities. Sixteen garden nodes cover all seven
raw materials across the four realms. Press E nearby to gather again;
gathering grants no XP and nodes remain available.

Lantern Weave adds 10 maximum mana per rank; Quiet Step reduces spell costs
by 5% per rank. Both have three ranks. Pack a Lumen Draught with P, close the
library, select the healing item with I and use F for up to 40 health.
Items are kept when the relevant resource is already full.

LOVE heals and lights the world. SPARK damages the training crystal.
AEGIS reduces incoming damage for six seconds. Item counts and spell costs
are shown in the HUD. Guides use authored fictional dialogue.

LOCAL SAVES
Default seed: 127. The version-independent save location is:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\HALVETH\PortalGarden\SaveGames\knowledge-SEED.json
For the default seed: knowledge-127.json.

Progression actions and periodic updates save learned pages, recipes, XP,
materials, packed consumables, enchantments, council choices and constructions
with their realm positions. F9 replaces in-memory progression with the stored
state. Save failures appear in the library. Incompatible or damaged files are
preserved; keep a backup before recovery.

A session begins in the Confluence. Position, current realm, current health,
mana, stamina, temporary effects and guide conversation steps are not resumed.
The shared save path is outside application-version folders. Future schema
compatibility must be checked; back up saves before testing a changed schema.

Detailed characters, animation, a campaign, voice, multiplayer and connected
AI conversations remain future development.
